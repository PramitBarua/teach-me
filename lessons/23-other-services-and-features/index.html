# Lesson Key idea
01AWS Local ZonesA Region extended near a metro area for extreme low latency; behaves like one AZ, no built-in resilience
Exam scope
The core fact to hold onto: a Local Zone is an extension of a parent Region, sited near a major metro area purely for low latency, and it behaves like a single Availability Zone — no built-in multi-AZ resilience. Recognize the naming pattern (parent Region + city code), and know the one-sentence distinction from its edge-family siblings: Local Zones extend a Region near a city, Wavelength sits inside telecom 5G networks, and Outposts brings AWS to your own data center.
