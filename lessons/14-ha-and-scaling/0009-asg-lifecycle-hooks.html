Auto Scaling Group Lifecycle Hooks

Lesson 09 of 12 · ~5 min

Source: Adrian Cantrill SAA-C03 · Amazon EC2 Auto Scaling Lifecycle Hooks

Normally, when an ASG decides to launch or terminate an instance, that happens with no opportunity for you to intervene. Lifecycle hooks insert a pause into that process — at launch, at termination, or both — giving you a window to run custom logic before the instance continues on its way.

The Extra States

TransitionNormal flowWith a lifecycle hook
LaunchPending → InServicePending → Pending:Wait → Pending:Proceed → InService
TerminateTerminating → TerminatedTerminating → Terminating:Wait → Terminating:Proceed → Terminated

An instance sits in the :Wait state until one of two things happens: a configurable timeout expires (3,600 seconds by default), or you explicitly call CompleteLifecycleAction once your custom logic has finished. On launch, a common use is loading or indexing data before the instance goes live. On termination, a common use is backing up logs or data before the instance disappears for good.

Exam tip
Lifecycle hooks integrate with EventBridge or SNS for transition notifications, enabling fully event-driven processing around instance launch and termination — this integration point is the detail most likely to appear as a specific exam answer option.
