Simple Storage Service (S3)

S3 Storage Classes — Part 2

Lesson 09 of 16 · ~10 min

Source: Adrian Cantrill SAA-C03 · S3 Storage Classes Overview

This lesson covers the Glacier tiers and Intelligent-Tiering. See Part 1 for Standard, Standard-IA, and One Zone-IA.

Glacier Instant Retrieval

Like Standard-IA but even cheaper storage with higher retrieval cost and longer minimums. Data is immediately retrievable (millisecond latency) but is designed for data accessed roughly once per quarter.

  • Minimum duration: 90 days
  • Use for: archive data you might need instantly but access very rarely

Glacier Flexible Retrieval

Objects are cold — not immediately accessible. You must submit a retrieval job; during retrieval the object is temporarily staged in Standard-IA. Objects cannot be made public.

Retrieval typeLatencyCost
Expedited1–5 minutesHighest
Standard3–5 hoursMid
Bulk5–12 hoursLowest
  • Minimum duration: 90 days · Minimum size: 40 KB
  • AZ count: 3 — same regional durability as Standard

Glacier Deep Archive

The coldest and cheapest storage class. Data is in a frozen state. Retrieval times are measured in hours to days.

Retrieval typeLatency
Standard~12 hours
BulkUp to 48 hours
  • Minimum duration: 180 days · Minimum size: 40 KB
  • Use for: regulatory archives, data retained for years but rarely if ever accessed

S3 Intelligent-Tiering

Automatically moves objects between tiers based on access patterns. A per-object monitoring and automation fee replaces retrieval fees.

TierMoves to this tier after
Frequent AccessDefault tier; also gets "viral" objects promoted back
Infrequent Access30 days of inactivity
Archive Instant Access90 days of inactivity (optional)
Archive Access90–270 days (optional, cold)
Deep Archive180–730 days (optional, cold)
Exam tip — quick decision guide
Know the access pattern? → Use the matching specific class (Standard, Standard-IA, Glacier, etc.).
Access pattern unknown or variable? → Use Intelligent-Tiering.

Retrieval Practice

2. You store medical scans in S3 Glacier Flexible. A scan from 3 years ago is needed urgently and must be available within 5 minutes. Which retrieval type should you request?

3. What fee does S3 Intelligent-Tiering charge instead of a retrieval fee?
