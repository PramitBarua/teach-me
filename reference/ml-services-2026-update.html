ML Services: What's Current for the Exam

Checked against the SAA-C03 exam guide v1.1 (published Oct 29, 2026) and AWS lifecycle pages · built 2026-11-07

Bottom line
The exam's ML list went from 11 services to 8. Forecast, Fraud Detector and Kendra were removed. The other 8 are still live; only some of their features changed. Study these 8 at "keyword → service" depth. Bedrock was not added: it appears on neither the in-scope nor the out-of-scope list.

SageMaker AI: what's still current

  • CurrentStudio, Canvas, JumpStart, training jobs, inference endpoints.
  • MaintenanceClosed to new customers Jul 30, 2026: A2I (human review), Clarify, Debugger, GeoSpatial, Ground Truth (labeling), Model Monitor, Role Manager, Studio Lab.
  • EndedGround Truth Plus (Jun 30, 2026). Profiler is in sunset.

Not worth study time

  • Officially out of scope: Personalize, HealthLake, Inferentia, Deep Learning AMIs/Containers, PyTorch/TensorFlow/MXNet on AWS.
  • On neither list: Bedrock (generative AI foundation models via API). Recognize the name as a distractor; don't study it. Amazon Q Business also moved to maintenance on Jul 30, 2026.
